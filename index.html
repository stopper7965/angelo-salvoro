      Before you ask

      How does ordering ahead work?

      Add drinks or beans to your order, choose a pickup time and send it to us by text or Messenger. We confirm right away and have it ready when you arrive. You pay at the counter.

      Can I really make my own latte art?

      Yes. Order any latte and ask the barista. They will guide your hand. Groups of four or more, please book ahead so we can give you the bar.

      Do you do coffee for weddings and company events?

      Yes. Mystique Flavors and Cafe Mystika Mobile bring a staffed coffee, cocktail or dry bar, from ₱10,500 for up to 30 guests. See every package, or text the date, venue and guest count for a quote the same day.

      Can you help me open my own cafe?

      Yes. Start with a Diagnostic Day for ₱7,500, or go straight to Cafe Setup and Launch. The Opening package bundles training, a costed menu, machine setup and your first beans for ₱55,000. See consultancy

      Do you sell beans and pastry wholesale?

      Yes. Thirteen roast profiles by the kilo with volume bands, plus muffins, bars, cookies and whole cakes on standing weekly orders. See trade prices
