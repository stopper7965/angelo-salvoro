      How to buy from us

      Nothing here needs a meeting first. We will visit if the job needs it.

      1. Send us what you need. Build a quote on this page, or text the shop name, town and what you are trying to fix.
      2. Get a written quotation. In pesos, with lead time and terms, the same day on most requests.
      3. Pay the way that works. Beans and pastry are payment first on the opening order, then terms after three clean cycles. Anything above PHP 7,000 splits into three.

      5. Training and events need a signed booking sheet and a deposit. Dates are not held on a verbal.
